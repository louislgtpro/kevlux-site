Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

In short: Kevlux collects no personal data, sends nothing over the internet and contains no ads or tracking tools.

Camera

Kevlux asks for camera access to measure light. The image is analyzed live, on the iPhone. No photo or video is saved to the photo library or transmitted: the RAW photo taken during a measurement is analyzed in memory and then deleted.

Data stored on the iPhone

The measurement history, camera profiles, light sources and settings are stored only on your iPhone, in the app’s own storage. They are neither synced nor sent, and they are deleted when you delete the app. They may be included in iPhone backups you make yourself (iCloud or computer), depending on your iOS settings.

No tracking

Kevlux uses no third-party service, no analytics or audience measurement tool, no advertising, no advertising identifier, and requires no account.

Apple

Downloads and updates go through Apple’s App Store, subject to Apple’s privacy policy. If you allow it in the iPhone’s settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with the developer.

Your rights

As Kevlux holds no data about you, no access or deletion request is needed: deleting the app erases everything. For any question: llegoutpro@gmail.com.
