How to measure

  • Place a gray card (or use a white wall) where the subject will be, facing the light, and fill the reticle.
  • Avoid reflections and your own shadow; hold the iPhone still for about a second after tapping “Measure”.
  • Without a reference, Scene mode estimates the light from the whole image: it is less reliable, and confidence never goes above “medium”.
  • Choose your target camera: the temperature is rounded to its step and the tint correction is given in its direction.

Frequently asked questions

Is the measurement accurate? It is indicative. The iPhone is not a calibrated colorimeter; the margin shown (±) is the spread of the frames, not a guaranteed accuracy. Always check the result on the camera.

Why is the tint direction sometimes “not documented”? Few manufacturers publish which way positive tint values go. Kevlux then shows which color to correct toward; the “Verify with Kevlux…” tool in the camera’s details lets you find out once and for all.

Is my data sent anywhere? No. See the privacy policy.
